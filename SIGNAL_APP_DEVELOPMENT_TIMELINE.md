# Signal App Development Timeline

Date: 2026-04-11

## Purpose

This document preserves the development arc of the MT-07 Live Signals app as reconstructed from:

- surviving repo files
- local git history
- preserved plan and checkpoint documents
- recovered Chrome download history

This is a reconstruction from surviving evidence, not a perfect forensic ledger. Even so, the line of development is clear.

## Governing Read

The MT-07 Live Signals app did not appear out of nowhere.

It emerged through a sequence of phases:

- symbolic and mythic interface experiments
- kernel and metric formalization
- audit and contract hardening
- transition into live geopolitical signal handling
- consolidation into a governed operator-facing application

## Interpreting the Arc

The strongest single read of the whole development arc is this:

- Myth-Tech began as symbolic and conceptual structure.
- That structure became measurable through metrics, ledgers, and audit artifacts.
- Those artifacts became governed through validators, checkpoints, and conformance logic.
- That governed kernel work was then translated into a live operational application.
- The resulting app became MT-07 Live Signals: an operator-facing field surface with audit lineage behind it.

## Why This Timeline Matters

This timeline proves several things:

- the Signal app has deep prehistory
- the myth layer and the app layer are historically connected
- auditability and structured reasoning were present long before the current repo state
- the present MT-07 app is an evolution of earlier Loom, kernel, and triad work rather than a disconnected rewrite
