# MT07 UAP App Concept

Date: 2026-04-29

## Status

This note defines the first concept shape for a UAP-focused app built with MT-07 signal logic.

This is a concept note, not an activation note.

Companion follow-on:

- [MT07_UAP_ANOMALY_WATCH_FIT_PLAN.md](./MT07_UAP_ANOMALY_WATCH_FIT_PLAN.md) maps this concept onto the current Live Signals repo architecture

It does not propose a truth engine.
It proposes a governed anomaly-monitoring system.

## Working Name

Preferred working name:

`MT07 Anomaly Watch`

Why:

- it sounds like a monitoring instrument, not a belief engine
- it keeps the app aligned with MT-07 operator logic
- it leaves room for both disclosure monitoring and anomaly-event tracking

## Governing Law

Do not ask the app to decide extraterrestrial truth.

Ask it to monitor anomaly claims, evidence quality, correction pressure, and disclosure discourse under load.

Companion line:

MT-07 remains sovereign over anomaly visibility, evidence weighting, correction pressure, corroboration logic, and operator-facing truth.

## Why This Fits MT-07

The current Signal app works because it does not pretend to know more than the field allows.

A UAP/UFO app would need the same discipline.

The field is full of:

- repost churn
- low-quality video
- weak provenance
- sensational framing
- misidentifications
- hoaxes
- real uncertainty
- intense narrative pressure

That makes it a good fit for MT-07 because MT-07 is strongest when:

- signal is noisy
- correction pressure matters
- source diversity must be separated from truth
- thin-sample honesty must remain visible

## Product Definition

This should not be framed as:

- `alien detector`
- `proof engine`
- `disclosure oracle`

It should be framed as:

- anomaly watch
- evidentiary pressure monitor
- disclosure and anomaly signal board

Tight definition:

The app monitors UAP-related discourse and anomaly claims while keeping uncertainty, provenance, and correction pressure visible.

## Core Lanes

The concept naturally splits into two lanes.

### 1. Disclosure / Discourse Lane

Use this lane for:

- government statements
- congressional hearings
- official briefings
- military and intelligence comments
- journalist and researcher reporting
- document release activity
- policy / oversight movement

This lane answers:

- is disclosure pressure rising
- is the topic becoming more institutionally visible
- are statements converging or conflicting
- is the discourse broadening, tightening, or fragmenting

### 2. Event / Anomaly Lane

Use this lane for:

- sighting reports
- photo/video claims
- witness accounts
- sensor or platform claims
- clustered local incidents
- repeated anomalies across time or region

This lane answers:

- what entered the field
- how strong is the provenance
- how independent are the sources
- what correction or debunk pressure exists
- what remains unresolved after ordinary explanations are considered

## MT-07 Governing Spine For This App

The UAP app should preserve the same general order:

`claim intake -> provenance check -> normalization -> incident form -> explanation pressure -> source-family separation -> corroboration pressure -> visibility gate -> thin-sample honesty -> metrics -> audit`

The important adaptation is that explanation pressure becomes central.

For this field, the app must constantly ask:

- drone?
- aircraft?
- satellite?
- launch?
- weather?
- astronomy?
- edited media?
- repost without provenance?

Not to collapse anomalies away by reflex.
But to keep the board honest.

## Proposed Metric Families

Good first metric candidates:

- `signal_velocity`
- `source_diversity`
- `correction_rate`
- `provenance_strength`
- `witness_independence`
- `explanation_pressure`
- `cross_source_coherence`
- `unresolved_anomaly_count`

Possible later metrics:

- `institutional_disclosure_pressure`
- `sensor_claim_density`
- `repeat-location_pressure`
- `video_repost_churn`

## Evidence Classes

The app will need stronger intake discipline than the current infrastructure board.

Suggested evidence classes:

- official statement
- official document
- journalist report
- researcher analysis
- first-hand witness report
- multi-witness report
- video/photo claim
- sensor claim
- repost / commentary only

This should feed directly into provenance and visibility logic.

## Non-Negotiable Boundaries

The app must not:

- declare extraterrestrial origin
- treat virality as corroboration
- treat many reposts as many witnesses
- treat video existence as evidentiary sufficiency
- publish claims to the board without provenance handling
- collapse “unexplained” into “proven”

The app may:

- mark unresolved anomalies
- mark rising disclosure pressure
- distinguish high-correction zones from stable ones
- show where the field is thin, noisy, or overconfident

## First MVP Shape

A strong first MVP would avoid the full anomaly lane at first.

Recommended MVP:

- disclosure/discourse monitoring
- curated anomaly report intake
- provenance-first event tape
- explanation-pressure field
- correction / debunk tracking
- no truth claims beyond governed field categories

This would let the app prove its law before carrying the hardest claims.

## Surface Modes

Likely modes:

- `live_api`
- `degraded`
- `mock`
- `empty`

Possible UAP-specific surface states:

- `thin_field`
- `repost_churn`
- `high_claim_low_provenance`
- `institutional_pressure_rising`
- `ordinary_explanation_available`
- `unresolved_but_unconfirmed`
- `calibration_pending`

## Best Fit For Claude / Helper Lanes

Helper lanes could fit well here, but only if they stay narrow.

Good helper roles:

- summarize discourse shifts
- surface correction patterns
- cluster repeated language
- compare stated claims vs visible evidence quality

Bad helper roles:

- deciding anomaly truth
- escalating low-provenance claims
- treating poetic summaries as evidentiary judgments

## Recommended Build Posture

Do not start with:

- public-facing grand claims
- automatic anomaly scoring from viral media alone
- open ingestion from uncontrolled social feeds

Start with:

- curated lanes
- provenance handling
- correction pressure
- explanation pressure
- thin-sample honesty

## Verdict

PASS, CONCEPT-STAGE

Why:

- the field is noisy enough to benefit from MT-07 discipline
- the current MT-07 logic is well suited to anomaly triage under pressure
- the concept is strongest when framed as monitoring and governed uncertainty, not belief

## Concept Seal

The app should not try to prove what UAPs are.

It should try to show:

- what entered the field
- how strong the evidence is
- how independent the sources are
- how much correction pressure exists
- what remains unresolved after ordinary explanation pressure is applied

## Tree of Relief

When the field is full of strange lights, the first duty is not wonder or denial, but lawful seeing.
