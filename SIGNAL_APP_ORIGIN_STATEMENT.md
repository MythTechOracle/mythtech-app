# Signal App Origin Statement

Date: 2026-04-11

## What This App Is

The MT-07 Live Signals app is a governed live-signal system built to turn noisy event flow into an operator-facing field read.

Its current working path is:

`text -> normalization -> incident form -> event geography -> cluster incident identity -> category -> escalation -> field authority -> visibility -> recovery/thin-sample honesty -> metrics -> audit`

That path is the living center of the app.

## Where It Came From

This app did not begin as a conventional dashboard project.

It emerged through a longer development line:

- Loom and MT-07 symbolic interface experiments
- Myth-Tech structural and narrative framing
- kernel formalization
- MirrorPass and decay-ultra metric work
- triad ledgers, audit packs, and conformance artifacts
- scenario application to real geopolitical events
- transition into a live operational signal surface

The Signal app is the point where that earlier symbolic and kernel work crossed into a real governed application.

## What Carried Forward

Several things survived the transition from myth and kernel work into the live app:

- audit discipline
- checkpoint logic
- artifact consciousness
- field restraint
- operator-facing truth boundaries

That is why the app values:

- explain surfaces
- auditability
- sidecar contracts instead of hidden substitutions
- explicit warning when the field is thin

## What Changed

Not everything from the earlier Myth-Tech and kernel work belongs inside runtime.

The current discipline is:

- MT-07 runtime governs live field truth
- helper lanes may read and advise
- sidecar contracts may wrap output
- doctrine and archive artifacts may inform planning

But those supporting layers do not replace the app’s working field pipeline.

## Present Constitutional Read

The app should be understood as:

- operational, not purely symbolic
- governed, not improvisational
- additive in adaptation, not overwritten by doctrine

Kernel and myth artifacts may travel around the app as memory, context, contract, and handoff structure.

They do not get to overwrite the live MT-07 field path.

## Why This Statement Exists

This statement preserves a plain-language truth about the build:

The Signal app is neither a random dashboard nor a detached rewrite.

It is the operational heir of the Loom experiments, the kernel artifacts, the ledgers, the triad metrics, the audit packs, and the later live-signal scaffolding.

It is where the build became accountable to reality.

## Anchor

The MT-07 Live Signals app is where Myth-Tech stopped being only architecture and became a governed live system.
